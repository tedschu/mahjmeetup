Add Seven Bam to your phone

Seven Bam runs in your phone's browser, and you can put it on your home screen so it opens in a single tap — no app store, nothing to install, nothing to update.

Step 1 — open the app first.
Tap Sign In / Create Account below. This matters: your home screen saves whatever page you are looking at, so adding it from this support page would save the website instead of the app.

Sign In / Create Account

Step 2 — add that page to your home screen.

iPhone or iPad
In Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen, scroll down, and choose Add to Home Screen. Tap Add.

Android
In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu in the top right, and choose Add to Home screen. Tap Add.

You stay signed in, so tapping the new icon takes you straight to your matches.

Common questions

How do I find a game?
Open Browse to see available tables and use filters to narrow your options.

How do I propose a match?
Choose Propose from Browse, then enter the date, time, venue, and any notes for players.

Where are my upcoming matches?
Open My Matches to see upcoming, hosted, and past games.

Where can I see league standings?
Open Leagues, select your league, and view standings or rankings.

How do I delete my account?
Open the Profile screen in Seven Bam and choose Delete my account. If you cannot reach your account, email sevenbamapp@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

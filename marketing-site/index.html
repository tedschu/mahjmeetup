FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Seven Bam?

Seven Bam is an app that helps players find local American Mahjong games, connect with other players, and manage leagues, matches, and open play

Do I need to be in a league to use it?

No. Seven Bam works for both social games and organized league play

Can I create my own game?

Yes. Hosts can post games, share details, and let other players join

Is Seven Bam only for experienced players?

No. It is for anyone who wants to find games, meet players, and play more
